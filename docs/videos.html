Watching these usefully

What to look for

  • One skill per turn. The robot acts, stops, and waits. Each pause is the model reading a result before choosing again.
  • Nothing is scripted. The sequences were composed at runtime from the skills in the library.
  • The pauses are the API. Latency between actions is the round trip to the model, not the robot thinking.
  • Drift accumulates. On longer runs the robot ends up somewhere other than where the maths says it should.
